# Product Marketing Masterclass Study Portfolio

Welcome to the **Product Marketing Masterclass Study Portfolio**—an exhaustive, high-density, and highly strategic study GitBook designed specifically for product marketing managers (PMMs), growth leads, and physical product founders.

This master portfolio compiles and synthesizes the core operational, strategic, and growth playbooks of two industry-defining masterpieces:

1. 🥇 **"Product-Led SEO"** by Eli Schwartz: The definitive guide to driving sustainable, programmatic growth by treating organic search as a product channel, aligning with Western user search intent.
2. 🥈 **"Ecommerce Evolved"** by Tanner Larsson: The definitive playbook for structuring, operating, and scaling a highly profitable, relationship-driven online brand, covering funnels, retention, inventory supply chain, and conversion stack optimizations.
