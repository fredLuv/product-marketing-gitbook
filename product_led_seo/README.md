# Part 1: Product-Led SEO (Eli Schwartz)

This section compiles the complete study guide for Eli Schwartz's industry-defining book: **"Product-Led SEO: The Why Behind Building Your Organic Growth Strategy."**

Treating search as a product channel allows e-commerce and technical companies to scale organic customer acquisition programmatically. Instead of writing blogs to chase competitors, you will design indexable support, review, and specification directories that capture high-intent search paths at the lowest Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
